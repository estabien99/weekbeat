## Open points

- **FIT pilates:** the SDK comments `subSport` pilates (44) with "Fitness Equipment"; we map `fitnessEquipment/pilates`.
  Garmin devices may write `training/pilates`. Check with real FIT files in v0.2. (confidence medium)
- **FIT HIIT:** the SDK has `sport` hiit (62) and `subSport` hiit (70). We map `training/hiit`. Check with real files.
- **Choice of Compendium row:** where several rows fit (e.g. weight training 3.5 vs 6.0 METs), the catalogue has one entry
  per effort level and the user picks. Freeletics sessions are mapped to an activity by the coach-plan connector (#9).
- **ACSM position stands** (2009 progression models, 2011 Garber et al.: rest between resistance sessions for the same
  muscle group, load increases) are the obvious primary sources for recovery gaps and strength progression, but
  PubMed and journals.lww.com could not be read from the research environment. Left out on purpose (user decision
  22.11.2026); add them with a verbatim quote once the text is available.
- **Weekly increase limit:** no sourced number. The "10 % rule" is not supported by the systematic review above, so
  the planner gets the principle only and offers increases, never forces them (user decision 22.11.2026).
- **Gaps after intense sessions, return after a break:** no rule yet (no primary source read). Until then the planner
  uses the WHO "start small" principle.
- **MTB → core, hip mobility, upper body:** no primary source found. Only the sourced MTB rule (strength training for
  neck, hand/wrist, low back, knees) is in v1.
- **Muscle groups:** none in the catalogue yet; no source checked. `rules.test.ts` keeps them empty until one is.
- **Impact** is set only for cycling, swimming (low) and jogging (medium). Walking, hiking, HIIT, strength and yoga have
  no impact level until a source classifies them.
- **Runners and strength training:** a 2024 meta-analysis on exercise-based prevention in endurance runners
  ([PMC11127851](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11127851/)) could not be read yet; candidate for a
  complement rule.
