## Open points

- **FIT pilates:** the SDK comments `subSport` pilates (44) with "Fitness Equipment"; we map `fitnessEquipment/pilates`.
  Garmin devices may write `training/pilates`. Check with real FIT files in v0.2. (confidence medium)
- **FIT HIIT:** the SDK has `sport` hiit (62) and `subSport` hiit (70). We map `training/hiit`. Check with real files.
- **Choice of Compendium row:** where several rows fit (e.g. weight training 3.5 vs 6.0 METs), the catalogue has one entry
  per effort level and the user picks. Freeletics sessions are mapped to an activity by the coach-plan connector (#9).
- Muscle groups, impact and complement rules (e.g. MTB → core, hip mobility) are **not** in v1 yet: they need their own
  sources (#3, part 2).
